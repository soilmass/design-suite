# ADR-0003 · Increase information density on the pricing table

## Context

The pricing table reads as sparse next to competitors that pack more plan
detail above the fold. F15 Density is tightly coupled to F16 and F33 per
Composition, so this cannot be decided for the pricing table alone without
also touching those.

## Decision

Proposed, not yet accepted: move F15.1 information density from "sparse"
toward the packed end, pending a joint pass on F16 and F33 as D001's
coupling rule requires.

## Consequences

None yet -- this is a proposal. F15 stays on default until F16 and F33 are
brought into the same sitting and an ADR is accepted.
